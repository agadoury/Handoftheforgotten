    Villages that worship you grow from Hamlet to Village to Town as they gain people and buildings; each step makes room for more houses and buildings. Builders use the storehouse’s wood, so uproot trees and bring it. Your creature earns XP from everything it does and gets a talent point each level; spend them in its mind, under Talents. Every talent changes how it looks, and the branch it leans into decides what it becomes. Food dropped on a storehouse feeds that village. Villages believe in you more when they see miracles, gifts and spectacle, and less when you or your creature hurt them.
